# Saman Devalaya

**District:** Ratnapura (Sabaragamuwa Province)
**Category:** religious site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** wet
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Hilltop temple of god Saman, guardian of Adam's Peak; striking river-side setting.

## Practical tips
- Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, and refrain from posing with your back to Buddha statues. Many sites do not allow drones.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Saman Devalaya via Ratnapura town. From Colombo it is roughly 100 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Ratnapura town.
