# Little Adam's Peak

**District:** Badulla (Uva Province)
**Category:** adventure / outdoor activity
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug, Sep
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Aug
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Easy 45-minute climb with classic Hill Country views; great for sunrise or sunset.

## Practical tips
- Tell someone your route, pack a head torch and starting before dawn beats both heat and the after-school crowds.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Little Adam's Peak via Badulla town. From Colombo it is roughly 220 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Badulla town.
