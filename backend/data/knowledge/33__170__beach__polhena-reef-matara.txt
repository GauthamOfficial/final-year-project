# Polhena Reef

**District:** Matara (Southern Province)
**Category:** beach / coastal experience
**Best months to visit:** Nov, Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar
**District peak season:** Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr
**Climate zone:** wet
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Calm shallow reef with excellent snorkelling; turtles graze on seagrass meadows.

## Practical tips
- Reef shoes help on rocky coves; surf currents can be strong, especially May-September on the south coast and December-March on the east.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Polhena Reef via Matara town. From Colombo it is roughly 160 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Matara town.
