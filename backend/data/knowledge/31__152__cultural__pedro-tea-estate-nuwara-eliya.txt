# Pedro Tea Estate

**District:** Nuwara Eliya (Central Province)
**Category:** cultural / heritage site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Aug
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 1000.00

## Overview
Working tea factory near Nuwara Eliya offering full process tours and high-grown tastings.

## Practical tips
- Bring water and a wide-brimmed hat; ancient cities offer little shade. Most sites accept photography for personal use without a fee.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Pedro Tea Estate via Nuwara Eliya town. From Colombo it is roughly 180 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Nuwara Eliya town.
