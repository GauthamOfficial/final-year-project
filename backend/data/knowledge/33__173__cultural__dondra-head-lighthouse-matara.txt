# Dondra Head Lighthouse

**District:** Matara (Southern Province)
**Category:** cultural / heritage site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr
**Climate zone:** wet
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 200.00

## Overview
49-metre lighthouse at Sri Lanka's southernmost point, built in 1890.

## Practical tips
- Bring water and a wide-brimmed hat; ancient cities offer little shade. Most sites accept photography for personal use without a fee.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Dondra Head Lighthouse via Matara town. From Colombo it is roughly 160 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Matara town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Dondra (Sinhala: දෙවිනුවර / දෙවුන්දර, romanized: Devinuvara / Devundara, Tamil: தேவேந்திரமுனை/தேவந்துறை, romanized: Tēvēntiramuṉai/Tevanturai) is a settlement on the extreme southernmost tip of Sri Lanka, in the Indian Ocean near Matara, Southern Province, Sri Lanka. The Dondra Head Lighthouse, ruins of several Hindu shrines of Tenavaram and a Vihara (Buddhist temple) are located in the vicinity.
