# Kudiramalai Point

**District:** Puttalam (North Western Province)
**Category:** cultural / heritage site
**Best months to visit:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**District peak season:** Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Red-cliff promontory with a cinematic baobab and crystal water; remote and lightly visited.

## Practical tips
- Bring water and a wide-brimmed hat; ancient cities offer little shade. Most sites accept photography for personal use without a fee.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Kudiramalai Point via Puttalam town. From Colombo it is roughly 135 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Puttalam town.
