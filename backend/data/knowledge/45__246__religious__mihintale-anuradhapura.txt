# Mihintale

**District:** Anuradhapura (North Central Province)
**Category:** religious site
**Best months to visit:** Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**District peak season:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 1000.00

## Overview
Cradle of Sri Lankan Buddhism — climb 1,840 steps to where King Devanampiya Tissa met Mahinda in 247 BC.

## Practical tips
- Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, and refrain from posing with your back to Buddha statues. Many sites do not allow drones.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Mihintale via Anuradhapura town. From Colombo it is roughly 205 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Anuradhapura town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Mihintale is a mountain peak near Anuradhapura in Sri Lanka.  It is believed by Sri Lankans to be the site of a meeting between the Buddhist monk Mahinda and King Devanampiyatissa which inaugurated the presence of Buddhism in Sri Lanka.  It is now a pilgrimage site, and the site of several religious monuments and abandoned structures.
