# Sinharaja Forest Reserve

**District:** Ratnapura (Sabaragamuwa Province)
**Category:** wildlife / safari experience
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug, Sep
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** wet
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 1500.00

## Overview
UNESCO-listed virgin lowland rainforest; biodiversity hotspot of the Indian Ocean.

## Practical tips
- Book a 4WD safari with a registered tracker. Mornings (06:00-09:30) and late afternoons (15:30-18:00) give the best wildlife viewing; midday is hot and animals retreat to shade.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Sinharaja Forest Reserve via Ratnapura town. From Colombo it is roughly 100 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Ratnapura town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Sinharaja Forest Reserve is the last virgin Rainforest left in Sri Lanka. Home to many rare endemic flora and fauna, it has been designated a World Heritage Site by UNESCO.
Formed during the Jurassic period (from 200 to 145 million years ago), Sinharaja forest is the most dense rain forest in Asia.
