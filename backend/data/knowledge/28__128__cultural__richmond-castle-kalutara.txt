# Richmond Castle

**District:** Kalutara (Western Province)
**Category:** cultural / heritage site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar
**Climate zone:** wet
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 1000.00

## Overview
1900s mansion combining British and Indian architecture, set in 100-year-old gardens.

## Practical tips
- Bring water and a wide-brimmed hat; ancient cities offer little shade. Most sites accept photography for personal use without a fee.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Richmond Castle via Kalutara town. From Colombo it is roughly 45 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Kalutara town.
