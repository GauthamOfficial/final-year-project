# Hambantota Salt Pans

**District:** Hambantota (Southern Province)
**Category:** cultural / heritage site
**Best months to visit:** Feb, Mar, Apr, May
**District peak season:** Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Vast geometric salt pans on the south-east coast — flamingos and kite-fliers in season.

## Practical tips
- Bring water and a wide-brimmed hat; ancient cities offer little shade. Most sites accept photography for personal use without a fee.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Hambantota Salt Pans via Hambantota town. From Colombo it is roughly 240 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Hambantota town.
