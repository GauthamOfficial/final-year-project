# Pahala Maharachchimulla Coconut Triangle

**District:** Kurunegala (North Western Province)
**Category:** cultural / heritage site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Heart of Sri Lanka's coconut belt — endless palm avenues and small cottage industries.

## Practical tips
- Bring water and a wide-brimmed hat; ancient cities offer little shade. Most sites accept photography for personal use without a fee.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Pahala Maharachchimulla Coconut Triangle via Kurunegala town. From Colombo it is roughly 95 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Kurunegala town.
