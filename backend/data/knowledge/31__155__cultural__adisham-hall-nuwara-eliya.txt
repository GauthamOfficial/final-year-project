# Adisham Hall

**District:** Nuwara Eliya (Central Province)
**Category:** cultural / heritage site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Aug
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 500.00

## Overview
1930s English country house turned Benedictine monastery; famed for its homemade jams and rose garden.

## Practical tips
- Bring water and a wide-brimmed hat; ancient cities offer little shade. Most sites accept photography for personal use without a fee.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Adisham Hall via Nuwara Eliya town. From Colombo it is roughly 180 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Nuwara Eliya town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Adisham (formerly Adesham) is a village and civil parish in the English county of Kent. It is twinned with Campagne-lès-Hesdin in France.
