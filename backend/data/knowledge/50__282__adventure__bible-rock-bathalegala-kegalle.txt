# Bible Rock (Bathalegala)

**District:** Kegalle (Sabaragamuwa Province)
**Category:** adventure / outdoor activity
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Flat-topped peak resembling an open book; 4-hour return hike with panoramic views.

## Practical tips
- Tell someone your route, pack a head torch and starting before dawn beats both heat and the after-school crowds.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Bible Rock (Bathalegala) via Kegalle town. From Colombo it is roughly 80 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Kegalle town.
