# Negombo Beach

**District:** Gampaha (Western Province)
**Category:** beach / coastal experience
**Best months to visit:** Nov, Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar
**District peak season:** Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar
**Climate zone:** wet
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Fishing-village beach 10 minutes from the international airport — handy first or last stop on any Sri Lanka trip.

## Practical tips
- Reef shoes help on rocky coves; surf currents can be strong, especially May-September on the south coast and December-March on the east.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Negombo Beach via Gampaha town. From Colombo it is roughly 30 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Gampaha town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Negombo (Sinhala: මීගමුව, romanized: Mīgamuva, IPA: [miːɡəmuʋə], Tamil: நீர்கொழும்பு, romanized: Nīrkoḻumpu, IPA: [n̪iːɾxoɻumbɯ]), also known as Punchi Romaya (Little Rome) and Meepura (city of bees), is a major city in Sri Lanka, situated on the west coast and at the mouth of the Negombo Lagoon, in the Western Province, 38 km (24 mi) from Colombo via the Colombo–Katunayake Expressway and the nearest major city from the Bandaranaike International Airport (about 9 km distance).
Negombo is a major commercial centre in the country and the second largest commercial centre in Gampaha District, and is also the administrative centre of Negombo Division.It is the second largest city in Gampaha District after Gampaha. Negombo has a population of about 142,136 within its divisional secretariat division. Negombo municipal boundary is fully extended throughout its Divisional Secretariat area.
Negombo is known for its long sandy beaches and centuries old fishing industry. Negombo has a large bilingual (Sinhala/Tamil) population with a clear Roman Catholic majority.
