# Muthiyangana Raja Maha Vihara

**District:** Badulla (Uva Province)
**Category:** religious site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Aug
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Ancient temple in central Badulla, said to mark a visit by the Buddha himself.

## Practical tips
- Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, and refrain from posing with your back to Buddha statues. Many sites do not allow drones.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Muthiyangana Raja Maha Vihara via Badulla town. From Colombo it is roughly 220 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Badulla town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Muthiyangana Raja Maha Vihara is an ancient Buddhist temple located in the middle of Badulla town in the Badulla District of Uva Province in Sri Lanka.
