# Rekawa Turtle Beach

**District:** Hambantota (Southern Province)
**Category:** wildlife / safari experience
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 1500.00

## Overview
Most consistent green-turtle nesting beach on the island; nightly guided walks year-round.

## Practical tips
- Book a 4WD safari with a registered tracker. Mornings (06:00-09:30) and late afternoons (15:30-18:00) give the best wildlife viewing; midday is hot and animals retreat to shade.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Rekawa Turtle Beach via Hambantota town. From Colombo it is roughly 240 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Hambantota town.
