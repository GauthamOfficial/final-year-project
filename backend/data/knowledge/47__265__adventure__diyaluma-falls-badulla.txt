# Diyaluma Falls

**District:** Badulla (Uva Province)
**Category:** adventure / outdoor activity
**Best months to visit:** Oct, Nov, Dec, Jan, Feb
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Aug
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
220-metre cascade — the country's second-tallest — with infinity-pool plunge pools at the top.

## Practical tips
- Tell someone your route, pack a head torch and starting before dawn beats both heat and the after-school crowds.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Diyaluma Falls via Badulla town. From Colombo it is roughly 220 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Badulla town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Diyaluma Falls is 220 m (720 ft) high and the second highest waterfall in Sri Lanka and 619th highest waterfall in the world. It is situated 6 km (3.7 mi) away from Koslanda in Badulla District on Colombo-Badulla highway. The falls are formed by Punagala Oya, a tributary of Kuda Oya which in turn, is a tributary of Kirindi Oya.
