# Aukana Buddha Statue

**District:** Anuradhapura (North Central Province)
**Category:** religious site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 500.00

## Overview
12-metre standing Buddha carved from a single granite outcrop in the 5th century.

## Practical tips
- Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, and refrain from posing with your back to Buddha statues. Many sites do not allow drones.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Aukana Buddha Statue via Anuradhapura town. From Colombo it is roughly 205 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Anuradhapura town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
The Avukana statue is a standing statue of the Buddha near Kekirawa in North Central Sri Lanka. The statue, which has a height of more than 14 m (46 ft), depicts the Buddha with a hand raised in reassurance, a variation of the Abhaya mudra. The Avukana statue is one of the best examples of a standing statue built in Sri Lanka. It is now a popular tourist attraction.
