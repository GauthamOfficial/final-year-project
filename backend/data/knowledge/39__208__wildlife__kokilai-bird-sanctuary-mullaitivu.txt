# Kokilai Bird Sanctuary

**District:** Mullaitivu (Northern Province)
**Category:** wildlife / safari experience
**Best months to visit:** Nov, Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar
**District peak season:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Coastal lagoon and mangroves hosting flamingos, herons and storks.

## Practical tips
- Book a 4WD safari with a registered tracker. Mornings (06:00-09:30) and late afternoons (15:30-18:00) give the best wildlife viewing; midday is hot and animals retreat to shade.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Kokilai Bird Sanctuary via Mullaitivu town. From Colombo it is roughly 335 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Mullaitivu town.
