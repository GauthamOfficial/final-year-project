# Lahugala-Kitulana National Park

**District:** Ampara (Eastern Province)
**Category:** wildlife / safari experience
**Best months to visit:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**District peak season:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 4500.00

## Overview
Small park where elephants gather to feed on tank-grass; quieter than Yala.

## Practical tips
- Book a 4WD safari with a registered tracker. Mornings (06:00-09:30) and late afternoons (15:30-18:00) give the best wildlife viewing; midday is hot and animals retreat to shade.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Lahugala-Kitulana National Park via Ampara town. From Colombo it is roughly 330 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Ampara town.
