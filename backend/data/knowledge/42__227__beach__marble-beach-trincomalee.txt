# Marble Beach

**District:** Trincomalee (Eastern Province)
**Category:** beach / coastal experience
**Best months to visit:** Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**District peak season:** Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Tranquil military-administered cove with calm water, picnic shelters and snorkelling.

## Practical tips
- Reef shoes help on rocky coves; surf currents can be strong, especially May-September on the south coast and December-March on the east.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Marble Beach via Trincomalee town. From Colombo it is roughly 260 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Trincomalee town.
