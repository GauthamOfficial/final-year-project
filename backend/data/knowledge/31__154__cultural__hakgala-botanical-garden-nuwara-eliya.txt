# Hakgala Botanical Garden

**District:** Nuwara Eliya (Central Province)
**Category:** cultural / heritage site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Aug
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 1500.00

## Overview
27-hectare highland garden under Hakgala rock, with rose terraces and cool-climate flora.

## Practical tips
- Bring water and a wide-brimmed hat; ancient cities offer little shade. Most sites accept photography for personal use without a fee.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Hakgala Botanical Garden via Nuwara Eliya town. From Colombo it is roughly 180 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Nuwara Eliya town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Hakgala Botanical Garden is one of the five botanical gardens in Sri Lanka. The other four are Peradeniya Botanical Garden, Henarathgoda Botanical Garden, Mirijjawila Botanical Garden and Seetawaka Botanical Garden. It is the second largest botanical garden in Sri Lanka. The garden is contiguous to Hakgala Strict Nature Reserve.
