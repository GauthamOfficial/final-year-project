# Yudaganawa Stupa

**District:** Moneragala (Uva Province)
**Category:** religious site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 200.00

## Overview
Massive 2nd-century BC stupa marking the battle between brothers Dutugamunu and Saddhatissa.

## Practical tips
- Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, and refrain from posing with your back to Buddha statues. Many sites do not allow drones.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Yudaganawa Stupa via Moneragala town. From Colombo it is roughly 240 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Moneragala town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Yudaganawa, is a town situated in Buttala Divisional Secretariat, Uva province, Sri Lanka.
