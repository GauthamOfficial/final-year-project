# Ravana Falls

**District:** Badulla (Uva Province)
**Category:** adventure / outdoor activity
**Best months to visit:** Oct, Nov, Dec, Jan, Feb
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Aug
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 200.00

## Overview
25-metre roadside waterfall named after the Ramayana king who allegedly hid Sita in nearby caves.

## Practical tips
- Tell someone your route, pack a head torch and starting before dawn beats both heat and the after-school crowds.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Ravana Falls via Badulla town. From Colombo it is roughly 220 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Badulla town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Ravana Ella, also known as Ravama Falls, or Bambaragala Falls (Sinhala: බඹරගල ඇල්ල, romanized: Bambaragala Ella) is a popular sightseeing attraction in Uva province of Sri Lanka. It currently ranks as one of the widest falls in the country.
