# Iranamadu Tank

**District:** Kilinochchi (Northern Province)
**Category:** wildlife / safari experience
**Best months to visit:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**District peak season:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Vast irrigation reservoir with birding hides and fishermen's catamarans.

## Practical tips
- Book a 4WD safari with a registered tracker. Mornings (06:00-09:30) and late afternoons (15:30-18:00) give the best wildlife viewing; midday is hot and animals retreat to shade.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Iranamadu Tank via Kilinochchi town. From Colombo it is roughly 330 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Kilinochchi town.

Iranamadu Tank (Tamil: இரணைமடு குளம் Iraṇaimaṭu Kuḷam; Sinhala: ඉරණමඩු වැව) is an irrigation tank in northern Sri Lanka, approximately 3 mi (5 km) south east of Kilinochchi.
