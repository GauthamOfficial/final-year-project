# Gangaramaya Temple

**District:** Colombo (Western Province)
**Category:** religious site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug, Dec
**District peak season:** Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** wet
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 500.00

## Overview
Eclectic urban temple complex blending Sri Lankan, Thai, Indian and Chinese architecture, with a museum of priceless jade and ivory donations.

## Practical tips
- Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, and refrain from posing with your back to Buddha statues. Many sites do not allow drones.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Gangaramaya Temple via Colombo town. From Colombo it is roughly 0 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Colombo town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Gangaramaya Temple (Sinhala: ශ්‍රී ගංගාරාම මහාවිහාරය śrī gangārāma mahāwihāraya, Tamil: ஸ்ரீ கங்காராம மகாவிகாரம் Srī Gaṅgārāma Makāvikāram) is one of the most important temples in Colombo, Sri Lanka, being a mix of modern architecture and cultural essence. Located on the Beira Lake, it was completed in the late 19th century.
