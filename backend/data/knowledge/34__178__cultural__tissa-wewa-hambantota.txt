# Tissa Wewa

**District:** Hambantota (Southern Province)
**Category:** cultural / heritage site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Ancient irrigation tank near Tissamaharama lined with palmyras — sunrise paddies and pelicans.

## Practical tips
- Bring water and a wide-brimmed hat; ancient cities offer little shade. Most sites accept photography for personal use without a fee.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Tissa Wewa via Hambantota town. From Colombo it is roughly 240 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Hambantota town.
