# Muthurajawela Wetland

**District:** Gampaha (Western Province)
**Category:** wildlife / safari experience
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar
**Climate zone:** wet
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 800.00

## Overview
Coastal marsh teeming with kingfishers, water monitors and crocodiles, explored by quiet boat.

## Practical tips
- Book a 4WD safari with a registered tracker. Mornings (06:00-09:30) and late afternoons (15:30-18:00) give the best wildlife viewing; midday is hot and animals retreat to shade.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Muthurajawela Wetland via Gampaha town. From Colombo it is roughly 30 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Gampaha town.
