# Henarathgoda Botanical Gardens

**District:** Gampaha (Western Province)
**Category:** cultural / heritage site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar
**Climate zone:** wet
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 600.00

## Overview
44-hectare gardens that pioneered Sri Lanka's rubber industry; oldest planted rubber tree on the island stands here.

## Practical tips
- Bring water and a wide-brimmed hat; ancient cities offer little shade. Most sites accept photography for personal use without a fee.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Henarathgoda Botanical Gardens via Gampaha town. From Colombo it is roughly 30 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Gampaha town.
