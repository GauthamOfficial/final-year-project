# Talaimannar Lighthouse

**District:** Mannar (Northern Province)
**Category:** cultural / heritage site
**Best months to visit:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**District peak season:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Whitewashed coastal beacon at the start of the bridge to India; sunset gold.

## Practical tips
- Bring water and a wide-brimmed hat; ancient cities offer little shade. Most sites accept photography for personal use without a fee.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Talaimannar Lighthouse via Mannar town. From Colombo it is roughly 320 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Mannar town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Talaimannar (Tamil: தலைமன்னார்; Sinhala: තලෙයිමන්නාරම) is a settlement in Sri Lanka located on the northwestern coast of Mannar Island.
