# Esala Perahera Procession Route

**District:** Kandy (Central Province)
**Category:** cultural / heritage site
**Best months to visit:** Jul, Aug
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Annual 10-night procession of caparisoned elephants and Kandyan dancers; tickets sell out months ahead.

## Practical tips
- Bring water and a wide-brimmed hat; ancient cities offer little shade. Most sites accept photography for personal use without a fee.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Esala Perahera Procession Route via Kandy town. From Colombo it is roughly 115 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Kandy town.
