# Mirissa Whale Watching

**District:** Matara (Southern Province)
**Category:** wildlife / safari experience
**Best months to visit:** Nov, Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr
**District peak season:** Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr
**Climate zone:** wet
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 6000.00

## Overview
Half-day boat trips that spot blue whales, sperm whales and acrobatic spinner dolphins.

## Practical tips
- Book a 4WD safari with a registered tracker. Mornings (06:00-09:30) and late afternoons (15:30-18:00) give the best wildlife viewing; midday is hot and animals retreat to shade.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Mirissa Whale Watching via Matara town. From Colombo it is roughly 160 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Matara town.
