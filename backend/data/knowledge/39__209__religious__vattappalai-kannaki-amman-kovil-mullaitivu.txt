# Vattappalai Kannaki Amman Kovil

**District:** Mullaitivu (Northern Province)
**Category:** religious site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Ancient seaside Hindu temple with an annual chariot festival drawing pilgrims from across the north.

## Practical tips
- Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, and refrain from posing with your back to Buddha statues. Many sites do not allow drones.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Vattappalai Kannaki Amman Kovil via Mullaitivu town. From Colombo it is roughly 335 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Mullaitivu town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Mullaitivu (Tamil: முல்லைத்தீவு, romanized: Mullaittīvu; Sinhala: මුලතිව්, romanized: Mulativ) is the main town of Mullaitivu District, situated on the north-eastern coast of Northern Province, Sri Lanka. A largely fishing settlement, the town in the early twentieth century grew as an anchoring harbour of the small sailing vessels transporting goods between Colombo and Jaffna. The town has a District Secretary's office, many other government institutions and schools located in and around the area.
