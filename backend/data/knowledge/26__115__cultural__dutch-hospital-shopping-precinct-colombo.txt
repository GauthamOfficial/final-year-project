# Dutch Hospital Shopping Precinct

**District:** Colombo (Western Province)
**Category:** cultural / heritage site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** wet
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Restored 17th-century hospital now hosting cafes, boutiques and rooftop bars.

## Practical tips
- Bring water and a wide-brimmed hat; ancient cities offer little shade. Most sites accept photography for personal use without a fee.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Dutch Hospital Shopping Precinct via Colombo town. From Colombo it is roughly 0 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Colombo town.
