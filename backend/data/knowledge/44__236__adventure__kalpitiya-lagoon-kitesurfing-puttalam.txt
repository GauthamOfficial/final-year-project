# Kalpitiya Lagoon Kitesurfing

**District:** Puttalam (North Western Province)
**Category:** adventure / outdoor activity
**Best months to visit:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**District peak season:** Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Flat-water kitesurfing paradise from May to September; beginners and pros alike.

## Practical tips
- Tell someone your route, pack a head torch and starting before dawn beats both heat and the after-school crowds.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Kalpitiya Lagoon Kitesurfing via Puttalam town. From Colombo it is roughly 135 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Puttalam town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Kalpitiya (Sinhala: කල්පිටිය, romanized: Kalpiṭiya, Tamil: கற்பிட்டி, romanized: Kaṟpiṭṭi) is a coastal town located in western region of Puttalam District. The Kalpitiya peninsula consists of a total fourteen islands. It is developing as a tourist destination.
