# Bahirawakanda Vihara Buddha Statue

**District:** Kandy (Central Province)
**Category:** religious site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 250.00

## Overview
26-metre seated Buddha overlooking Kandy from the western hills; sunset views over the lake.

## Practical tips
- Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, and refrain from posing with your back to Buddha statues. Many sites do not allow drones.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Bahirawakanda Vihara Buddha Statue via Kandy town. From Colombo it is roughly 115 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Kandy town.
