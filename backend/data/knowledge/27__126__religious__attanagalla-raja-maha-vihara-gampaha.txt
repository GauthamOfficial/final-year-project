# Attanagalla Raja Maha Vihara

**District:** Gampaha (Western Province)
**Category:** religious site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar
**Climate zone:** wet
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Ancient royal temple linked to King Walagamba; Bo tree, dagoba and a peaceful tank.

## Practical tips
- Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, and refrain from posing with your back to Buddha statues. Many sites do not allow drones.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Attanagalla Raja Maha Vihara via Gampaha town. From Colombo it is roughly 30 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Gampaha town.
