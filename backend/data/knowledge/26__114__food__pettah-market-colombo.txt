# Pettah Market

**District:** Colombo (Western Province)
**Category:** food / culinary experience
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** wet
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Sprawling commercial bazaar with spice, fruit, fabric and electronics streets.

## Practical tips
- Carry small denominations of LKR for tickets and tips.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Pettah Market via Colombo town. From Colombo it is roughly 0 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Colombo town.
