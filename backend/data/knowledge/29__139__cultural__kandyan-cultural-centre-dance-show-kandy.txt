# Kandyan Cultural Centre Dance Show

**District:** Kandy (Central Province)
**Category:** cultural / heritage site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 1500.00

## Overview
Nightly showcase of Kandyan dance, fire-walking and traditional drumming.

## Practical tips
- Bring water and a wide-brimmed hat; ancient cities offer little shade. Most sites accept photography for personal use without a fee.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Kandyan Cultural Centre Dance Show via Kandy town. From Colombo it is roughly 115 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Kandy town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Kandyan dance (Sinhala: උඩරට නැටුම්) encompasses various dance forms popular and native to the area called Kandy of the Central Hills region known as Udarata in Sri Lanka, which have today spread to other parts of the country.
It is an example and considered a masterpiece and a sacred artwork in Sri Lanka.
