# Kudumbigala Forest Monastery

**District:** Ampara (Eastern Province)
**Category:** religious site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 500.00

## Overview
Forest monastery on a granite outcrop with cave hermitages and a hilltop dagoba; meditative views over Yala.

## Practical tips
- Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, and refrain from posing with your back to Buddha statues. Many sites do not allow drones.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Kudumbigala Forest Monastery via Ampara town. From Colombo it is roughly 330 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Ampara town.
