# Nallur Kandaswamy Kovil

**District:** Jaffna (Northern Province)
**Category:** religious site
**Best months to visit:** Aug, Sep
**District peak season:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Vast saffron Hindu temple at the heart of Jaffna; 25-day August festival climaxing in chariot processions.

## Practical tips
- Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, and refrain from posing with your back to Buddha statues. Many sites do not allow drones.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Nallur Kandaswamy Kovil via Jaffna town. From Colombo it is roughly 400 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Jaffna town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
The Nallur Kandaswamy Kovil  (Tamil: நல்லூர் கந்தசுவாமி கோவில், romanized: Nallūr kantacuvāmi kōvil Sinhala: නල්ලුරුව ස්කන්ධ කුමාර කෝවිල, romanized: nalluruva skandha kumāra kōvila) is a Hindu temple, located in Nallur, Northern Province, Sri Lanka. The presiding deity is Murugan (Kartikeya) in the form of the divine spear vel in the sanctum, the primary shrine, and in other forms, namely, Shanmugar, Muthukumaraswami, Valli Kanthar with consorts Valli and Deivayanai, and Dandayuddhapani, sans consorts in secondary shrines in the temple.
