# Hummanaya Blowhole

**District:** Matara (Southern Province)
**Category:** adventure / outdoor activity
**Best months to visit:** Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar
**District peak season:** Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr
**Climate zone:** wet
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 200.00

## Overview
Second-largest sea blowhole in the world, sending water 25 m skyward on swelly days.

## Practical tips
- Tell someone your route, pack a head torch and starting before dawn beats both heat and the after-school crowds.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Hummanaya Blowhole via Matara town. From Colombo it is roughly 160 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Matara town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Hummanaya Blowhole is the only known blowhole in Sri Lanka and it is considered to be the second largest blowhole in the world. Hummanaya (Sinhala: හුම්මානය = හූ+මානය) refers to the noise, "hoo", that can be heard a distance away when the blowhole is active.
