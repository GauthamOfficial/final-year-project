# Akkarayan Tank

**District:** Kilinochchi (Northern Province)
**Category:** wildlife / safari experience
**Best months to visit:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**District peak season:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Quiet tank on the road to Mullaitivu — kingfishers, lily blooms and palmyra forest.

## Practical tips
- Book a 4WD safari with a registered tracker. Mornings (06:00-09:30) and late afternoons (15:30-18:00) give the best wildlife viewing; midday is hot and animals retreat to shade.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Akkarayan Tank via Kilinochchi town. From Colombo it is roughly 330 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Kilinochchi town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Kilinochchi District (Tamil: கிளிநொச்சி மாவட்டம் Kiḷinocci Māvaṭṭam; Sinhala: කිලිනොච්චි දිස්ත්‍රික්කය) is one of the 25 districts of Sri Lanka, the second level administrative division of the country. The district is administered by a District Secretariat headed by a District Secretary (previously known as a Government Agent) appointed by the central government of Sri Lanka. The capital of the district is the town of Kilinochchi.
