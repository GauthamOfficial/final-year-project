# Arankele Forest Monastery

**District:** Kurunegala (North Western Province)
**Category:** religious site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 200.00

## Overview
Tranquil 6th-century forest monastery on a wooded hillside; ruined meditation paths and ponds.

## Practical tips
- Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, and refrain from posing with your back to Buddha statues. Many sites do not allow drones.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Arankele Forest Monastery via Kurunegala town. From Colombo it is roughly 95 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Kurunegala town.
