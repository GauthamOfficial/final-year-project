# Ridi Vihara

**District:** Kurunegala (North Western Province)
**Category:** religious site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 250.00

## Overview
Silver-mine cave temple with intricate Kandyan-period murals.

## Practical tips
- Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, and refrain from posing with your back to Buddha statues. Many sites do not allow drones.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Ridi Vihara via Kurunegala town. From Colombo it is roughly 95 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Kurunegala town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Ridi Viharaya (Sinhala: රිදී විහාරය) or Silver Temple is a 2nd-century BCE Theravada Buddhist temple in the village of Ridigama, Sri Lanka. Built during the reign of Dutthagamani of Anuradhapura, the temple is considered as the place where the silver ore, which provided silver to complete Ruwanwelisaya; one of the largest stupas in Sri Lanka, was discovered. According to the chronicles Mahavamsa and Thupavamsa, the Ridi Viharaya complex was built in gratitude for helping him cherish his dream of completing Ruwanwelisaya.
