# Medirigiriya Vatadage

**District:** Polonnaruwa (North Central Province)
**Category:** religious site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 500.00

## Overview
Hidden 8th-century circular shrine on a forested hill, three concentric stone columns surrounding the dagoba.

## Practical tips
- Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, and refrain from posing with your back to Buddha statues. Many sites do not allow drones.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Medirigiriya Vatadage via Polonnaruwa town. From Colombo it is roughly 215 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Polonnaruwa town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Medirigiriya Vatadageya (Sinhala: මැදිරිගිරිය වටදාගෙය) is a Buddhist structure (Vatadage) in Medirigiriya, Sri Lanka. The structure has been formally recognised by the Government as an Archaeological Protected Monument.
