# Nayaru Lagoon Beach

**District:** Mullaitivu (Northern Province)
**Category:** beach / coastal experience
**Best months to visit:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**District peak season:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Empty crescent of white sand where a lagoon meets the sea; one of Sri Lanka's wildest beaches.

## Practical tips
- Reef shoes help on rocky coves; surf currents can be strong, especially May-September on the south coast and December-March on the east.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Nayaru Lagoon Beach via Mullaitivu town. From Colombo it is roughly 335 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Mullaitivu town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Mullaitivu District (Tamil: முல்லைத்தீவு மாவட்டம், Mullaittīvu Māvaṭṭam; Sinhala: මුලතිවු දිස්ත්‍රික්කය, Mulativu distrikkaya) is one of the 25 districts of Sri Lanka, the second level administrative division of the country. The district is administered by a District Secretariat headed by a District Secretary (previously known as a Government Agent) appointed by the central government of Sri Lanka. The capital of the district is the town of Mullaitivu.
