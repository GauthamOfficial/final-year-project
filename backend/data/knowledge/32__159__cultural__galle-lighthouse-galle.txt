# Galle Lighthouse

**District:** Galle (Southern Province)
**Category:** cultural / heritage site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Nov, Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar
**Climate zone:** wet
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
26-metre lighthouse inside Galle Fort; built by the British in 1939, still operational.

## Practical tips
- Bring water and a wide-brimmed hat; ancient cities offer little shade. Most sites accept photography for personal use without a fee.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Galle Lighthouse via Galle town. From Colombo it is roughly 120 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Galle town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
The Galle Lighthouse (also known as Pointe de Galle Light) is an onshore lighthouse in Galle, Sri Lanka and is operated and maintained by the Sri Lanka Ports Authority. It is Sri Lanka's oldest light station.
