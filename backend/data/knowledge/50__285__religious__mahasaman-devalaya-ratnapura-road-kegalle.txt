# Mahasaman Devalaya, Ratnapura Road

**District:** Kegalle (Sabaragamuwa Province)
**Category:** religious site
**Best months to visit:** Apr, Aug
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Old-world devalaya complex hosting a colourful July-August perahera.

## Practical tips
- Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, and refrain from posing with your back to Buddha statues. Many sites do not allow drones.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Mahasaman Devalaya, Ratnapura Road via Kegalle town. From Colombo it is roughly 80 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Kegalle town.
