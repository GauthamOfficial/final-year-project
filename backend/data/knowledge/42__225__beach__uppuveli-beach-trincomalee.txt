# Uppuveli Beach

**District:** Trincomalee (Eastern Province)
**Category:** beach / coastal experience
**Best months to visit:** Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**District peak season:** Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Quieter cousin of Nilaveli with simple beach guesthouses and fresh seafood.

## Practical tips
- Reef shoes help on rocky coves; surf currents can be strong, especially May-September on the south coast and December-March on the east.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Uppuveli Beach via Trincomalee town. From Colombo it is roughly 260 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Trincomalee town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Uppuveli (Tamil: உப்புவெளி) is a gold sand coastal resort town of the Trincomalee District, Sri Lanka located in the suburb of Sambalativu, 6 km northwest of the city of Trincomalee.  A historically popular tourist destination of the island alongside the nearby Nilaveli, the numbers of visitors declined following the 2004 Indian Ocean Tsunami and Sri Lankan Civil War, but have risen again since 2010. It is home to the Salli Muthumariamman Kovil on its coast.
