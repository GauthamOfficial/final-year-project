# Bopath Ella Falls

**District:** Ratnapura (Sabaragamuwa Province)
**Category:** adventure / outdoor activity
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** wet
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
30-metre falls shaped like a Bo leaf; popular swimming pool at the base.

## Practical tips
- Tell someone your route, pack a head torch and starting before dawn beats both heat and the after-school crowds.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Bopath Ella Falls via Ratnapura town. From Colombo it is roughly 100 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Ratnapura town.
