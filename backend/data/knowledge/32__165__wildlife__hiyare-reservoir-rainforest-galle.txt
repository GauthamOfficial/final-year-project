# Hiyare Reservoir & Rainforest

**District:** Galle (Southern Province)
**Category:** wildlife / safari experience
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**District peak season:** Nov, Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar
**Climate zone:** wet
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 600.00

## Overview
Quiet inland reservoir with a research station, rare endemic frogs and easy lakeside trails.

## Practical tips
- Book a 4WD safari with a registered tracker. Mornings (06:00-09:30) and late afternoons (15:30-18:00) give the best wildlife viewing; midday is hot and animals retreat to shade.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Hiyare Reservoir & Rainforest via Galle town. From Colombo it is roughly 120 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Galle town.
