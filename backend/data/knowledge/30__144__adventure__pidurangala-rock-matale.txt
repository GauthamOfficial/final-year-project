# Pidurangala Rock

**District:** Matale (Central Province)
**Category:** adventure / outdoor activity
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**District peak season:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 1000.00

## Overview
Adjacent rock with the iconic sunrise view of Sigiriya from the recumbent Buddha summit.

## Practical tips
- Tell someone your route, pack a head torch and starting before dawn beats both heat and the after-school crowds.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Pidurangala Rock via Matale town. From Colombo it is roughly 140 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Matale town.
