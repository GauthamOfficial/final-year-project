# Casuarina Beach

**District:** Jaffna (Northern Province)
**Category:** beach / coastal experience
**Best months to visit:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**District peak season:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Shallow turquoise water on Karainagar island, a 45-minute drive from Jaffna town.

## Practical tips
- Reef shoes help on rocky coves; surf currents can be strong, especially May-September on the south coast and December-March on the east.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Casuarina Beach via Jaffna town. From Colombo it is roughly 400 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Jaffna town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Casuarina Beach (also Casoorina, Cashoorina) is in Karainagar, Jaffna District, Sri Lanka, about 20 kilometres (12 mi) from Jaffna. The 1.5 km long white sand beach is considered to be the best beach in the Jaffna Peninsula. The beach is named after the Casuarina trees that grow on the shore.
