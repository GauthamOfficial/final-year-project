# Panduwasnuwara Royal Citadel

**District:** Kurunegala (North Western Province)
**Category:** cultural / heritage site
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 500.00

## Overview
12th-century capital with palace ruins, a temple of the tooth and a royal swimming pool.

## Practical tips
- Bring water and a wide-brimmed hat; ancient cities offer little shade. Most sites accept photography for personal use without a fee.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Panduwasnuwara Royal Citadel via Kurunegala town. From Colombo it is roughly 95 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Kurunegala town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Panduwasnuwara is an ancient capital, situated in Kurunegala District, Sri Lanka. It is said to be the controlling centre known as Parakramapura of Dakkhinadesa (South Country) in the 12th century, when it was ruled by Parakramabahu. The remaining ruins of the ancient kingdom still can be seen at Kotampitiya area which lies along Wariyapola-Chilaw main road about 19 km (12 mi) away from Wariyapola town. Panduvasnuwara is located at a distance of 3 km from Hettipola and 18 km from Kuliyapitiya.
