# Ritigala Forest Monastery

**District:** Anuradhapura (North Central Province)
**Category:** religious site
**Best months to visit:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**District peak season:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee (LKR, indicative):** 1500.00

## Overview
Mountain monastery in dense jungle with paved meditation paths and stone double-platforms.

## Practical tips
- Cover shoulders and knees, remove shoes and hats, and refrain from posing with your back to Buddha statues. Many sites do not allow drones.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Ritigala Forest Monastery via Anuradhapura town. From Colombo it is roughly 205 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Anuradhapura town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Ritigala is a mountain in central Sri Lanka which is home to an ancient Buddhist monastery. The ruins and rock inscriptions of the monastery date back to 1st century BCE. It is located 43 km (27 mi) away from the ancient monastic city of Anuradhapura.
