# Whiskey Point

**District:** Ampara (Eastern Province)
**Category:** beach / coastal experience
**Best months to visit:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**District peak season:** May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep
**Climate zone:** dry
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Mellow beach break 10 minutes north of Arugam Bay — the local longboard hangout.

## Practical tips
- Reef shoes help on rocky coves; surf currents can be strong, especially May-September on the south coast and December-March on the east.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Whiskey Point via Ampara town. From Colombo it is roughly 330 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Ampara town.

## Background (excerpted from Wikipedia)
Arugam Bay (Tamil: அறுகம் குடா, romanized: Aṟukam kuṭā; Sinhala: ආරුගම් බොක්ක, romanized: ārugam bokka), known locally as "Arugam Kudah", is situated on the Indian Ocean in the dry zone of Sri Lanka's southeast coast, and a historic settlement of the ancient Batticaloa Territory (Mattakallappu Desam). Arugam Kudah's literal Tamil translation is "Bay of Cynodon dactylon".
