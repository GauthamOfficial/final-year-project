# Single Tree Hill

**District:** Nuwara Eliya (Central Province)
**Category:** adventure / outdoor activity
**Best months to visit:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Jul, Aug
**District peak season:** Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Aug
**Climate zone:** intermediate
**Entry fee:** Free

## Overview
Easy 90-minute climb to a 360-degree viewpoint over Nuwara Eliya valley.

## Practical tips
- Tell someone your route, pack a head torch and starting before dawn beats both heat and the after-school crowds.

## How to get there
Most travellers reach Single Tree Hill via Nuwara Eliya town. From Colombo it is roughly 180 km by road; public buses run daily from Pettah, and intercity trains serve the major hill-country and coastal districts. Tuk-tuks cover the last mile from Nuwara Eliya town.
