Mirissa — Whales, Surf, and the Southern Coast
==============================================

Mirissa is a small fishing town and beach resort on Sri Lanka's southern coast, in the Matara District of the Southern Province. It became internationally known in the early 2000s as a launching point for blue whale watching and has since developed into a balanced beach destination popular with backpackers and families alike.

Whale Watching
--------------

The continental shelf drops sharply just a few kilometres south of Mirissa, bringing deep, krill-rich water within easy reach of small boats. Between November and April, blue whales — the largest animals on the planet — migrate through these waters, often accompanied by sperm whales, fin whales, and several dolphin species. Tours depart at 06:30 from Mirissa harbour and last 3-5 hours. Look for operators that follow the SLTDA whale-watching code of conduct (boats must remain at least 100 metres from any cetacean).

Beaches and Surf
----------------

The main beach is a wide crescent of golden sand bordered by a promontory called Coconut Tree Hill, which is a popular sunset photograph location. Beginner surfers practise on the small, gentle break in front of the main strip, while more experienced riders head 8 km west to Weligama Bay. The water can be deceptively strong during the southwest monsoon (May–September) — swim only between the lifeguard flags.

Food and Nightlife
------------------

Seafood is the local specialty: line-caught tuna, kingfish, prawns, and small reef snapper appear on every beachside menu. Several restaurants serve their catch as Sri Lankan-style "devilled" preparations or simple grills with rice and curry. Mirissa has the most active nightlife on the south coast outside Galle, with beachfront bars hosting fire shows and DJ sets through the high season.

Getting There
-------------

The Southern Expressway shortens the drive from Colombo to Mirissa to roughly 2.5 hours; alternatively the coastal train from Colombo Fort to Weligama (followed by a short tuk-tuk ride) is one of the most scenic rail journeys in Asia.
